The first 12 months

Measured, in three stages.

0–90 DAYS

Learn the processes. Document what's ad hoc. Build the templates. No new tools yet.

MONTHS 3–6

A reviewed email workflow — faster replies, still checked by a person before sending.

MONTHS 6–12

Propose voice support for after-hours calls — a recommendation for you to approve, not a rollout.
